Family-run in Jaipur since 2006

The same family, the same yard in Transport Nagar, since 2006.

SureRide is a family business, not an app. Our own cars, our own drivers, and a phone answered in Jaipur by the person who owns them.

It started with one Indica and one driver taking families to Khatu Shyam and back. Twenty years on there are eighteen vehicles in the yard, all of them ours, and eleven drivers who have been with us long enough that regular passengers ask for them by name.

We kept doing one thing the same way the whole time: the fare is agreed before the car leaves, in writing, and it does not change when you arrive. No festival pricing, no rate that goes up because the highway was busy. If the fare has to change — a route you added, a night that became two — we tell you before it happens, not on the invoice.

Most of our work still comes the old way — a family who travelled with us last year telling their relatives. The rest now find us on their phones, which is why this site exists: so the fares, the cars and the drivers are all written down before you call.

About the drive

It is NH-48 the whole way — Jaipur to Delhi with no diversions, four to six lanes, in good condition. Leaving Jaipur at 6:30 in the morning you are usually at South Delhi by half past eleven. Leave at nine and Gurgaon traffic adds an hour at the far end; leaving after four in the afternoon means arriving in the evening rush, which we will tell you honestly when you call.

There are four toll plazas between Jaipur and the Delhi border — Manoharpur, Shahjahanpur, Daruhera and Kherki Daula. Our drivers stop once, usually at the Behror dhabas about two hours in, where the Rajasthani thali is good and the washrooms are clean enough for a family. If you would rather stop earlier, Midway Manoharpur is about ninety minutes out.

For a round trip the same driver stays with you in Delhi and brings you back — you are not handed to a second car. Airport drops go to T1, T2 or T3 as you need, and the driver waits at the pillar number you send him.

Every driver holds a commercial licence and a valid police verification, kept on file at our Jaipur office. On a round trip the same driver stays with you for the whole journey. You are never handed over to another car. Long drives are planned with a rest break. We would rather leave an hour earlier than push a tired driver.
